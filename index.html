PROJECT // 01 Aerospace & Defence · Digital Twin

AeroSim — Aircraft Component Failure Prediction

"Predict Aircraft Component Failures Before They Happen."

Engineered during the 36-hour T-Hub × Crescent Institute BUILDX Hackathon 2026 (Nov 4–5, 2026, Chennai). AeroSim is an engineering digital twin platform that models individual aircraft components (engine mounts, landing gears, turbine blades, hydraulic actuators) using physics-based degradation equations (fatigue, crack growth, thermal creep, seal wear) and flight telemetry. Features a controlled two-stage update method: Stage A operational model correction and Stage B inspection-confirmed damage correction.
